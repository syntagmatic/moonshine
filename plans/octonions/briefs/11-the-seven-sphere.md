# 11 — The Seven-Sphere: S⁷

## Pitch
Spheres ($S^n$) are normally just sets of points. But $S^0, S^1, S^3,$ and $S^7$ are special: they are the only spheres that carry a group-like multiplication. This explainer explores the $S^7$ born from Octonions.

## Figures
1. **The Multiplication Sphere.** Highlight $S^0, S^1, S^3, S^7$ on the "cliff" map.
2. **Rotating S⁷.** A projection of an 8D sphere rotation using Octonions. Interactive.
3. **The Hopf Fibration.** A mention of the $S^7 \to S^4$ map with an $S^3$ fiber. Static.

## Key formulas / constructions
- The set of unit Octonions $\{ x \in O : |x| = 1 \}$ forms $S^7$.
