# 09 — Triality and Spinors

## Pitch
The Octonions create a 3-way symmetry in 8D: between vectors, left-handed spinors, and right-handed spinors. This "Triality" is unique to 8D. It explains why Octonions are the bridge to string theory and unified physics.
