# 10 — Moufang Loops

## Pitch
Without associativity, $(ab)c \neq a(bc)$. But Octonions aren't "lawless." This explainer introduces the Moufang Loops, the specific kind of structure that survives non-associativity.

## Figures
1. **The Moufang Identity Check.** Visualizing $(a(bc))a = (ab)(ca)$. Even when standard associativity fails, this identity holds. Interactive.
2. **The Diassociative Property.** Show that any two octonions generate an associative sub-algebra (the Quaternions). The reader "chooses" two octonions and sees they act like H. Interactive.

## Key formulas / constructions
- Moufang Identity: $(a(bc))a = (ab)(ca)$
- Diassociativity: Any subalgebra generated by two elements is associative.
