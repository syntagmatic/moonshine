# 14 — Freudenthal’s Magic Square

## Pitch
This is the "Periodic Table" of exceptional symmetry. By combining two algebras—one for the rows and one for the columns—we generate all the major Lie groups. The corner of the square is $E_8$. This explainer shows how the Octonions act as the "engine" for the most complex symmetries in math.

## Figures
1. **The Magic Square Table.** A 4x4 grid of $R, C, H, O$. Hovering over a cell (e.g., $O \times O$) highlights the resulting group ($E_8$) and its dimension (248). Interactive.
2. **The Dimension Map.** A bar chart comparing the sizes of the groups in the square.
3. **The exceptional chain.** E6 → E7 → E8. Showing how they "grow" as we move along the Octonion row.

- Dimensions: $F_4(52), E_6(78), E_7(133), E_8(248)$.
