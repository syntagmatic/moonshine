|---|---|---|---|---|
| 02 | Shoreline Amphitheatre "opened in 1987" | wrong, fixed | Shoreline opened in 1986 (first show summer 1986, Julio Iglesias, after the Dead's booked opening was cancelled when Garcia fell into a coma); the Dead first played it 1987-10-03. Wikipedia "Shoreline Amphitheatre"; Ticketmaster venue history | "opened in 1986; the Dead first played it in 1987 and had 39 shows there through 1995" |
| 01 | "The Arena years (1978 to 1986) settled the show into the two-set format with Drums and Space in the middle of the second" | wrong, fixed | The two-set-plus-encore show was standard from 1971: the 1970 acoustic/electric three-set shows ended when the acoustic sets stopped in 1971, and from late 1971 shows were two electric sets (deadessays.blogspot.com, "The Dead's Acoustic Sets 1969-1970"; Wikipedia, 1970-72 live releases). What the late 1970s fixed was Drums and Space in Set 2: 03's own counts (Drums at 34 of 41 shows in 1976; Space 44/81 in 1978, 79/87 in 1980) | "The two-set show was already the norm, and the Arena years (1978 to 1986) fixed Drums and Space in the middle of the second set" |
| 03 | "by 1972 that shape was more common than not" (format section) and "After the 1975 hiatus the two-set-plus-encore format settled in" (shape section) | wrong, fixed | as above; the two statements also contradicted each other | Format section: "The acoustic sets stopped in 1971, and by the end of that year the two-set shape was the norm." Shape section: "though two sets and an encore were already the rule. After the 1975 hiatus the middle of Set 2 settled in." |
