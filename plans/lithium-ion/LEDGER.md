# Claims ledger: lithium-ion

This series hasn't had a full fact-check; these are settled leads only (from plans/FACT-CHECK.md, 2026-09-27).

| page | claim | verdict | source or derivation | fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 03 | SEI activation energy "reported in the 40-60 kJ/mol range" (uncited); code uses 50 kJ/mol | unverifiable as worded, fixed with sources | Waldmann et al., J. Power Sources 262, 129 (2014): Ea = 0.38 eV (about 37 kJ/mol) for cycle aging of NMC/graphite 18650s above 25 C. Schmalstieg et al., J. Power Sources 257, 325 (2014): calendar fade factor proportional to exp(-6976/T), i.e. Ea about 58 kJ/mol. 50 kJ/mol sits inside that span | Parenthetical now cites both and says the page uses 50 |
| 03 | 25 to 45 C "multiplies k by about 4", "about four times sooner", caption "roughly quadruples" | wrong, fixed | exp(50000/8.314 (1/298.15 - 1/318.15)) = 3.55; 25 to 35 C gives 1.92 ("roughly doubles" is fine); sqrt(3.55) = 1.88 | Prose now "about 3.5" and "about 3.5 times sooner"; Figure 1 caption now "multiplies it by about 3.5" and thickness "about 1.4x and 1.9x" |
| 03 | A grid battery doing hundreds of full-depth cycles "is cycle-dominated" | wrong against the page's own model, fixed | Ran Li.aging.duty in node: Grid-tied preset (25 °C, 50% SOC, 0.4 DOD, 365/yr) gives 3.69% calendar vs 1.02% cycle at year 5, 78% calendar; full depth 365/yr gives 48% calendar, level | Sentence now says the Grid-tied preset still loses about three quarters of its fade to the calendar and a full-depth daily cycle only brings cycle fade level |
| 02 | At low current η_ct dominates "because Butler-Volmer is already steep near i ≈ i0" | wrong reason, fixed | Near i0 Butler-Volmer is linear: R_ct = RT/(F i0) = 0.02569/0.6 = 43 mΩ·m² at 25 °C with the page's I0_REF, vs R_OHM = 18 mΩ·m²; η_diff's linear resistance RT/(F i_lim) is under 2 mΩ·m² | Now says the interface acts as a 43 mΩ·m² resistor, more than twice the ohmic 18 |
| 02 | η_ct "settles on the double-layer RC in about a second" | wrong, fixed | Part 4's default Randles values R_ct = 0.15, C_dl = 10^-1.3 = 0.05 give R_ct C_dl = 7.5 ms | "in milliseconds (R_ct C_dl = 7.5 ms with Part 4's default Randles values)" |
| 01 | On a plateau "a millivolt of noise can move the estimate by tens of percent of SOC" | overstated, fixed | Ran Li.ocv.dVdx.LFP over x = 0.1..0.9: 0.16 to 0.44 mV per %SOC, so 2.2 to 6.2 %SOC per mV (full-cell LFP plateau similar, up to 6.1) | "a single millivolt ... moves the estimate by 2 to 6 percent of SOC on the LFP plateau, and a few millivolts of error is worth tens of percent" |
| 01 | NMC full cell is "a gentle S-curve from 4.2 to 3.3 V" | inconsistent with the series, fixed | Li.cell.ocvSoc for NMC gives 4.19 V at SOC 1 and 2.88 V at SOC 0 (X_EMPTY 0.14, X_FULL 0.895) | "falls from about 4.2 to 2.9 V across the cell's usable window" |
| 03 | Df ≈ 1.71 "for 2-D off-lattice clusters, the Witten-Sander result, consistent with the branched morphology seen in cryo-EM of plated lithium" | wrong attribution and wrong morphology claim, fixed (from memory of the literature, not re-fetched) | Witten and Sander, PRL 47, 1400 (1981) simulated DLA on a square lattice; about 1.71 is the off-lattice value from Meakin (PRA 27, 1495, 1983) and later large runs (Tolman and Meakin, PRA 40, 428, 1989: 1.715). Li et al., Science 358, 506 (2017) cryo-EM shows plated Li as single-crystal nanowires (whiskers), not fractal clusters | Credits Witten and Sander with the lattice model and 1.71 to later off-lattice simulations; says cryo-EM shows whiskers and mossy deposits, so DLA captures the tip instability, not the shape |
| 03 | "EVs are often set to stop at 70%" | wrong, fixed (from general knowledge of OEM defaults) | Common daily charge-limit guidance is 80 to 90% (e.g. Tesla's app defaults and recommends a daily limit up to 90%; many OEMs suggest 80%) | "80 or 90% for daily use" |
