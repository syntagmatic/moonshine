## Errors confirmed and fixed

Items marked **new** were not in the review. I found them while checking.

**exceptional-atlas**

- 01 (old 02): the proof that the root inner product is positive definite was
  invalid ("positive diagonal, integer off-diagonals"). I replaced it with the
  standard argument (Humphreys §8.5): κ(h,h′) = Σ α(h)α(h′) gives (λ,λ) = Σ(α,λ)²,
  and integrality makes the form rational.
- 02: "a few dozen reflections" is now 120, the length of the longest element.
- 03: old 04 and old 05 contradicted each other on which Cartan entry is larger.
  I computed B₃ and G₂ with lib/lie-math.js and stated the rule once:
  A_ij ∈ {−2, −3} exactly when α_i is long and α_j is short.
- 03, **new**: 05's symmetry identity (α_i,α_i)A_ij = (α_j,α_j)A_ji was backwards.
  The correct identity is (α_j,α_j)A_ij = (α_i,α_i)A_ji.
- 03: the D_n bound is now n ≥ 4 and the C_n bound n ≥ 3. The low-rank
  coincidences are stated.
- 04, **new**: the derivation of 1/p + 1/q + 1/r > 1 was wrong. It claimed
  "(P,P) > 0 rearranges to Σ1/p > 1", which would actually give Σ < 3. I replaced it
  with Humphreys' argument: the squared cosines from the branch node to the three
  orthogonal arm vectors sum to less than 1.
- 04: rewrote the garbled enumeration table.
- 04: "E₈ is the largest in the ADE family" now reads "largest exceptional".
- 04: "sum < 1 is hyperbolic or Lorentzian" now says exactly one negative
  eigenvalue, by interlacing; E₁₀ = (7,3,2) is hyperbolic. I did not carry over
  old 12's claim that the fake monster Lie algebra is hyperbolic Kac-Moody.
- 04, **new**: Borel–de Siebenthal. The page claimed every node deletion from Ẽ₈
  gives a maximal subalgebra. Only nodes with prime marks do. The subalgebras
  A₁+A₇, A₁+A₂+A₅ and D₅+A₃ are not maximal.
- 05: "every a_ij ∈ {0,1}" fails for ℤ/2, where V = 2·sign gives a double edge (Ã₁).
- 06: the mixed octonion units have exactly three imaginary ±½ entries, not
  "three or four".
- 07: "E₆ is the smallest algebra containing the Standard Model" is wrong. SU(5)
  is smaller, E₆ is the largest of the three, and its 27 splits as 16 + 10 + 1.
- 07: "three 27s, same object" is replaced by the two exact links. The Albert
  algebra is the 27 of E₆(−26), and the weights of the 27 are the lines of a
  cubic surface as a W(E₆)-set.
- 07, **new**: "24-cell, the only self-dual regular 4-polytope" is wrong, since the
  5-cell is also self-dual. It is the only self-dual regular non-simplex in
  dimension 3 or higher.
- 07, **new**: F₄'s middle double edge is bounded by positivity, not by valency.
- 07: the isometry group of OP² is F₄ and its collineation group is E₆(−26). The
  article states both correctly.
- 08: "30 is the same as the number of rings" is fixed. There are 8 rings of 30,
  30 is the Coxeter number, and 8 × 30 = 240. I verified this by projection; the
  radii pair off in golden ratio, as checked numerically.
- 08, **new**: the face-count table listed cross-polytope 4- and 5-faces. All faces
  below the facets are simplices.
- 08, **new**: the histogram status said "±2 columns contain 120 pairs each". The
  +2 column is empty.
- 08 (from PC-16): PC-16 said both exponent sets are H₄'s exponents. Only
  {1, 11, 19, 29} is; {7, 13, 17, 23} is its image under ×7 mod 30.
- 08 (from PC-16): stage 3 said "inner (φ⁻¹) and outer (φ)". The radii are
  √(1 ∓ 1/√5), a ratio of φ.
- 08 (from 10b): the central claim was false. The 120 even plus 120 odd pinors of
  Cl(3) under the Euclidean norm form two orthogonal 600-cells (they have inner
  products of 0.309), not E₈. The article now says so and points to the
  golden-field form of the icosians.
- 09: the kissing table claimed the LP bound is tight in d = 3 and d = 4. It is
  13.16 and 25.56 there; the bound is tight only in 1, 2, 8 and 24. I fixed the
  table, the chart data and the prose.
- 09: "Construction A on Golay gives 194,352 minimal vectors" is wrong. Scaled
  unimodular, it has 48 minimal vectors of norm 2 (the Niemeier lattice A₁²⁴).
  Construction B is now described correctly.
- 09: Co₂ and Co₃ stabilise vectors of type 2 and type 3 (norms 4 and 6), not

**parallel-coordinates**

- 06, **new**: the stated theorem was false. A point's polyline staying inside the
  band between the axes is necessary for containment but not sufficient. For
  example, (7,3) lies outside the circle of radius 2.5 centred at (5,5), yet its
  segment stays inside the band on [0,1]. The correct test is the separating-line
  argument: the whole line y(t) = (1−t)p₁ + t·p₂, for every real t, must stay in
  the band.
- 06: the union/intersection duality was reversed. P ∈ S₁∩S₂ exactly when its line
  lies in both bands. When S₁∩S₂ ≠ ∅, the band of conv(S₁∪S₂) is the union of the
  two bands.
- 06: "inside iff within both pairwise hstars" is false in 3D. The pairwise test is
  necessary, and sufficient for boxes. Counterexample: (4,6,4) lies outside the
  page's tetrahedron but passes both pairwise tests; a 0.5 grid has 194 such points.
- 06: the polygon paragraph is fixed. The band's edges are vertex lines, and its
  corners sit at the dual points of the polygon's edges.
- 12 (old 13): "which polylines cross between which axes is edge incidence and a
  topological invariant" is false. Two polylines cross between axes i and i+1
  exactly when their i-th and (i+1)-th coordinates are in opposite order. I
  removed the figure built on the claim (its "Topology preserved" label) and wrote
  a correct paragraph.
- 12 (old 13), **new**: the 24-cell was said to have 6-fold symmetry in the zw
  plane. With the page's coordinates it is 4-fold in every coordinate plane.
- 12: "the only regular self-dual non-simplex" now carries its dimension condition.
  I removed "PC views are always distinct", "full combinatorial signature" and "a
  self-dual polytope has a symmetry swapping vertices and cells".

## Figures made honest or removed

- EA-01: the Killing-form tables were hand-typed matrices. One figure now builds
  the ad matrices for sl₂, sl₃ and sl₄, takes the traces, checks them against the
  sum over roots, computes det κ, and inverts for the simple-root Gram matrix.
- EA-03: the delete-node figure used a hardcoded table of names and root counts.
  It now filters the 240 roots and names the components from the diagram.
- EA-03: the angle picker's ratio slider could never reach √2 or √3, so the B₂ and
  G₂ configurations were unreachable. The slider now sets the squared ratio.
- EA-05: the McKay quivers, irrep dimensions and tensor formulas were drawn by
  hand, and the "side-by-side identity check" drew the same graph twice and always
  said "Identical". The page now generates each group from quaternions, finds its
  classes, finds its irreducible characters by tensoring with V and splitting
  constituents, computes a_ij, and runs an isomorphism test against the
  independently drawn affine diagram. All cases match, and the dimensions equal the
  marks.
- EA-06: the 240-integral-octonion figure used a "synthetic layout" with
  hardcoded counts. It now searches the 30 Fano planes for orders closed under
  multiplication (57,600 products checked; 7 of the 30 close), reports the inner
  products, and derives the 2+126+112 split. The magic square is computed from the
  Tits dimension formula. Removed: the zero-divisor minefield, the S⁷ navigator
  and all "Mission" framing.
- EA-09: the Viazovska toy was wrong in three ways. It used 1D Fourier
  transforms for an 8D problem, it checked f ≤ 0 only at 4 radii, and it reported
  f(0)/f̂(0), which is not the bound. Its "magic" point fails the real conditions,
  and the family has no feasible point in 8D. I replaced it with an exact 8D
  Cohn–Elkies family whose best bound is 2.005 × E₈.
- EA-08: removed a `found < 120` cap from the H₃ pair-product figure (the count
  really is 120). The figure now reports the inner products.
- PC-06: containment colour came from Cartesian tests, and the unused
  `segmentInsideEnvelope` was itself wrong. The page now implements the real
  extended-line test, with a live grid check: 1681/1681 agreement, while the
  between-axes-only test wrongly accepts 62 points. The 3D figure reports the
  pairwise test against the barycentric truth.
- PC-12: symmetry detection was hardcoded. It now compares rotated and original
  vertex sets.
- PC-12: dropped the tesseract → 16-cell "morph" and the deformation figure.
