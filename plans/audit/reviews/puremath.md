**Series.** Acts I-III (01-12) are a competent Serre/Zagier-style walk. The best pages have real computing figures (reduction to F, Hecke T_p, F_p point counts, the tau mod 691 table). But three pages restate the dimension formula, and several standard facts are wrong in ways a number theorist spots at once (integrality of E_k, generators over Z, Hecke/Mordell dates, Ribet vs Serre). Act III's close (13-14) is filler with a fake figure. Act IV is where the series earns its name and where it is least reliable. 17-18 invent structure (E8^3 inside Leech, a "four-step bridge", a made-up Coxeter projection) and would embarrass the gallery in front of anyone who knows moonshine. 19-20 are salvageable. Craft is uniform "Play: / Takeaways" scaffolding with a chatty intro paragraph bolted onto each terse body. The two voices are visibly different, which suggests layered generation. Recommended shape: about 13 articles. Fold 02+05 and 06+09, cut 13, 14, 17 and 18, rewrite Act IV as 15 j / 16 Monster / 19 FLM / 20 Borcherds, and move the Conway-Norton spec into 19.

**Series.** It has the most ambitious figures in the group, and they compute: coboundary matrices, Z/2 Gaussian elimination, the cup product via Alexander-Whitney, and discrete Stokes. Worth keeping with a serious fix pass: repair 05's cover logic and Betti numbers, redo 06 over Z/p, strip the dossier references and grand summaries, and absorb TDA 01 as a prelude.

## information-geometry (15 articles, ~6.4k words; avg 430 words)

The metric reports 0 controls because figures are built through IG.viz.figure and d3.drag. **Real figures do exist.** Sliders in 01, 05, 06, 08 and 10; drag handles elsewhere. lib/infogeo-math.js (700 lines) computes Gaussian Fisher, closed-form KL, e/m geodesics, Bregman, NGD vs SGD, and a real EM. I checked 08: the status bar checks KL(P||R) = KL(P||Q) + KL(Q||R) for Q = (mu_P, sigma0), and that identity is exact. 13 has no figure, only a table. 15's j heatmap truncates the q-series at 4 terms down to Im tau = 0.25, where it is badly inaccurate.
