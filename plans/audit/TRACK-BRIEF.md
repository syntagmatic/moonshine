# Track brief: restructure one series cluster

I'm cutting this gallery down to work that meets a Distill.pub bar. Most of it
was batch-generated by an older model. Each series was reviewed, and the
verdicts are in `plans/audit/reviews/` (ranking in `plans/audit/CUTS.md`).
Your track owns one cluster of series. The job is to leave it smaller, correct
and honest.

## Setup (you are in your own git worktree)

- `docs/vendor/` is gitignored, so link it in before anything renders:
  `ln -s /Users/kai/git/moonshine/docs/vendor docs/vendor` (it is excluded
  locally, so it won't be committed).
- To serve, run `python3 -m http.server <port> -d docs` on the port your
  prompt gives you. Pages that `fetch()` fail over `file://`.
- Read `AGENTS.md` for the repo conventions, and read the Anti-Slop section
  of `plugins/moonshine/SKILL.md`.

## Order of work

1. **Cut and merge.** Start from the reviewer's per-article verdicts. You
   may disagree with one, but record why. Delete cut pages outright. A merge
   means rewriting two articles into one, not concatenating them. Renumber the
   survivors contiguously and update every internal link.
2. **Keep the good interactives.** Before deleting a page, check whether it
   has a figure that computes something real and teaches well. If it does,
   give it a home in a surviving article of your track. If nothing fits,
   write it up in your findings file (source path, what it does, suggested
   home). Don't edit `plans/salvage/`, which I maintain.
3. **Check every flagged error before fixing it.** Derive it, compute it,
   or check a primary source. Reviewers make mistakes too. Fix only the
   errors you confirm, and record the ones you reject, with the reason.
4. **Honest figures.** If a figure fakes computation (hardcoded results,
   hand-typed "derived" data, `Math.random` standing in for real data, a check
   that always passes), make it compute the real thing. If that isn't
   feasible, remove the figure and the prose claims that depended on it.
   Never relabel a fake as "illustrative" and keep it.
5. **Prose you write or rewrite** follows the anti-slop rules: no em dashes,
   no "not X but Y", no grand summaries, no callout or KPI boxes. Leave
   paragraphs you didn't otherwise touch alone. A full prose pass is a later
   phase.
6. **Series index.** Update the cards, intro prose and footer so they match
   what survives.

## Boundaries

- Edit only files under your series directories, plus your findings file.
- Don't edit `docs/index.html` (the homepage) or other series. Grep all of
  `docs/` for links into pages you deleted or renamed, and list them in your
  findings file. I fix them at merge time.

## Gate before committing

- Run `node ~/.claude/scripts/render-check.mjs` over HTTP on every page in
  your series, and all must pass.
- For every figure you changed, drive its main interaction headlessly
  (Playwright: drag, slide or click, whatever a reader would do), and confirm
  it has no errors and that the display changes.
- Commit on your worktree branch, staging only your own files. Don't merge
  and don't push.

## Findings file: `plans/audit/findings/<track>.md`

- What was cut, merged and renumbered (old to new filename)
- Errors confirmed and fixed; errors rejected, with the reason
- Figures made honest or removed
- Unresolved items that need a human
- Inbound links from other series that now point at deleted or renamed pages
- The homepage entry to use: title, count, desc, tags

## Report back

At most 400 words: branch name, final commit, article count before and after,
the most important fixes, anything unresolved. Everything else goes in the
findings file.
