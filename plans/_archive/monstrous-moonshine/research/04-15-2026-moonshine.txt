The phenomenon, coined "Monstrous Moonshine" by John Conway and Simon Norton in 1979, originated from a simple but baffling numerical observation that completely defied standard algebraic paradigms. The term "moonshine" was explicitly chosen by Conway to reflect the seemingly absurd, foolish, or far-fetched nature of the connection. As number theorist Don Zagier later recalled, the connection appeared so unreasonable that it bordered on mathematical hallucination. Yet, the subsequent decades of research proved the coincidence to be the surface manifestation of an incredibly deep geometric reality.
