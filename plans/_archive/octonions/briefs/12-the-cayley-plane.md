# 12 — The Cayley Plane: OP²

## Pitch
Standard projective geometry works in any dimension for Reals and Complexes. But for Octonions, you can only build a "line" and a "plane." This explainer introduces the Cayley Plane ($OP^2$), a 16-dimensional geometric object that is the most symmetrical plane in existence.

## Figures
1. **The Projective Line (S⁸).** Visualizing $OP^1$ as an 8-dimensional sphere. Static/SVG.
2. **The 16D Plane Projection.** A 3D slice of the 16-dimensional geometry of the Cayley Plane. Drag to rotate. Interactive.
3. **The Symmetry Scorecard.** Comparing $RP^2, CP^2, HP^2,$ and $OP^2$. Highlighting why $OP^n$ stops at $n=2$.

## Key formulas / constructions
- The Cayley Plane: $F_4 / Spin(9)$
- Dimension: 16
