# 08 — The G2 Symmetry Group: Octonion Automorphisms

## Pitch
This is the birth of the "Exceptional Groups." A group of rotations in 7D that preserve the Octonion multiplication table. G2 is the smallest exceptional group, and it exists because Octonions are non-associative.

## Figures
1. **The Automorphism Test.** Pick a rotation. Does it "break" the Fano Plane? Most do. The ones that don't are G2. Interactive.
2. **The 14-Dimensional Group.** Visualizing why G2 is 14-dimensional (picking an $e_1$, then an $e_2$, then an $e_4$).
3. **The G2 Mandala.** A specific projection of G2's root system, echoing the E8 mandala. SVG.

## Key formulas / constructions
- Automorphism condition: $f(xy) = f(x)f(y)$
- $G_2$ dimension: 14
