## Quote Clusters By Concept

### Gravity / Grace / Void

Use *Gravity and Grace* to connect `pesanteur`, imagination, void, and grace. The page should paraphrase the surrounding notebook cluster: gravity pulls mechanically; imagination fills voids; grace is not produced by effort.

Short fragments to verify:

- "laws analogous to physical gravity"
- "Grace is the only exception"
- "beliefs which fill up voids"

### Attention / Waiting / Neighbor

Use the school-studies essay for attention as disciplined emptiness, and the Bousquet letter for attention as generosity. The connection to another person should matter as much as the connection to truth.

Short fragments to verify:

- "suspending our thought"
- "substance of prayer"
- "What are you going through?"

### Force / War

Use the Iliad essay as the main source. Keep the language sober: force makes persons thing-like, but this is not moral equivalence between aggressor and victim.

Short fragments to verify:

- "the true subject ... is force"
- "makes a thing"
- "nobody really possesses it"

### Oppression / Bureaucracy / Technique

Use *Oppression and Liberty* for social machinery, revolution, technocracy, and means/end reversal. The key bridge is the separation of thought from action.

Short fragments to verify:

- "separation between thought and action"
- "relationship between means and ends"
- "thinking clearly"

### Work / Factory / Method

Use factory writings for fatigue, humiliation, rhythm, and obedience. Use *The Need for Roots* to preserve the positive side of work: work can be contact with necessity when method and attention remain joined.

Short fragments to verify:

- "count for nothing"
- "daily death"
- "spirituality of work"

### Liberty / Obligation / Roots

Use *The Need for Roots* for obligation, rootedness, and reconstruction. The final page should not end with hagiography; it should hold the political insight and the reception problem together.

Short fragments to verify:

- "obligations comes before ... rights"
- "real, active, and natural participation"
- "needs of the soul"
