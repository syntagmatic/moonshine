# Source Map

## Primary Works And Where They Serve The Series

### *Gravity and Grace* / *La pesanteur et la grace*

Use for gravity, grace, void, decreation, contradiction, detachment, imagination, beauty, metaxu, the social imprint, and the Great Beast.

Edition caution: posthumous topical selection from notebooks arranged by Gustave Thibon. Do not present it as a book Weil designed.

Working web anchors:

- Wikiquote, *Gravity and Grace*: https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Gravity_and_Grace
- Routledge edition anchor: https://www.routledge.com/Gravity-and-Grace/Weil/p/book/9780415290005

### *Waiting for God* / *Attente de Dieu*

Use for attention, waiting, school studies, prayer, obedience, neighbor-love, affliction, and consent.

Edition caution: posthumous collection of letters and essays. Separate the school-studies essay, letters to Perrin, and reflections on affliction when citing.

Working web anchors:

- Matheson Trust excerpt, "Reflections on the Right Use of School Studies": https://www.themathesontrust.org/library/weil-reflections-on-the-right-use
- Wikiquote, Simone Weil, school-studies section: https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Simone_Weil

### Letter To Joe Bousquet / Correspondence

Use for attention as generosity. This is a concise source for attention as a relation to another person, not only an intellectual practice.

Working web anchor:

- Quotepark source note for the Bousquet letter: https://quotepark.com/quotes/1536018-simone-weil-attention-is-the-rarest-and-purest-form-of-generos/

### "The Iliad, or the Poem of Force"

Use for force, war, thingification, victory, prestige, and the deformation of both victims and agents.

Edition caution: English translation wording varies. The essay is outside the two organizing titles but required for Act II.

Working web anchors:

- Wikiquote, Simone Weil, Iliad section: https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Simone_Weil
- Wikipedia overview and source history: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Iliad_or_the_Poem_of_Force
- The Anarchist Library text locator: https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/simone-weil-the-iliad

### *Oppression and Liberty*

Use for oppression, social machinery, revolution, bureaucracy, technocracy, power, means/end reversal, and liberty as thought joined to action.

Edition caution: posthumous political collection, centered on "Reflections Concerning the Causes of Liberty and Social Oppression."

Working web anchors:

- Routledge edition anchor: https://www.routledge.com/Oppression-and-Liberty-1st-Edition/Weil/p/book/9780415254076
- Goodreads quote locator, to verify against edition: https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/1260403-r-flexions-sur-les-causes-de-la-libert-et-de-l-oppression-sociale

### Factory Writings / *La condition ouvriere*

Use for factory labor, rhythm, humiliation, obedience, fatigue, separation of thought and action, and the bridge to `malheur`.

Edition caution: use direct factory writings rather than biographical summaries when building pages.

Working web anchors:

- EBSCO overview of *Factory Journal*: https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/history/factory-journal-simone-weil
- SimoneWeil.net factory-work locator: https://simoneweil.net/factorywork.htm
- Goodreads quote locator for *La Condition ouvriere*: https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/313843-la-condition-ouvri-re

### *The Need for Roots* / *L'enracinement*

Use for liberty, obligation, rootedness, uprootedness, work, needs of the soul, reconstruction, truth, and anti-hagiographic caution.

Edition caution: late wartime political writing, posthumously published; do not reduce it to inspirational rootedness.

Working web anchors:

- Routledge edition anchor: https://www.routledge.com/The-Need-for-Roots-Prelude-to-a-Declaration-of-Duties-Towards-Mankind/Weil/p/book/9780415271011
- Wikipedia overview: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Need_for_Roots
- Goodreads quote locator, to verify against edition: https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/313866-l-enracinement-pr-lude-une-d-claration-de-devoirs-envers-l-tre-huma
