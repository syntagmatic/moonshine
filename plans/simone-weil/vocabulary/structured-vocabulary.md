## II. Emmy Noether: The Vocabulary of Structural Revolution

### Core Mathematical Operators
- **Noetherian** — A ring or module satisfying the ascending chain condition; finiteness captured structurally. *Signature concept.*
- **Ideal** — A subset of a ring closed under absorption by multiplication; the lens through which modern algebra views divisibility.
- **Symmetry** — A transformation preserving structure. Noether's theorem reveals symmetry as the *source* of conservation laws.
- **Isomorphism** — Equality not of elements but of structural relations.
- **Abstract Algebra** — The study of operations themselves, divorced from their material instantiations.

### Metamathematical Stance
- **Denken statt Rechnen** ("Thinking instead of calculating") — Noether's credo: conceptual clarity over algorithmic manipulation.
- **Structural Insight** — The leap from computation to the recognition of underlying form.
- **Axiomatic Purity** — Foundations built from minimal assumptions, revealing what is essential.

---

## III. André Weil: The Vocabulary of Mathematical Architecture

### Foundational Constructs
- **Weil Conjectures** — Deep hypotheses linking the number of solutions to polynomial equations over finite fields to topology; proven by Deligne, they reshaped algebraic geometry.
- **Bourbaki** — The pseudonymous collective André co-founded to reconstruct mathematics from set-theoretic foundations upward; emblematic of *structuralist* ambition.
- **Adele / Idele** — Topological-algebraic structures unifying local and global number theory.
- **Uniformization** — The program of reducing complex geometries to simpler, universal forms.

### Epistemic Modes
- **The Analogy** — André's characteristic method: perceiving hidden correspondences between number theory and geometry.
- **The Foundational Urge** — The drive to rebuild mathematics from first principles, eliminating intuitive sloppiness.
- **Mathematical Taste** — The cultivated intuition for which problems are worthy and which paths lead to essence.

## IV. Simone Weil: The Vocabulary of Spiritual Ontology

### Core Philosophical Operators
- **Attention** — Not concentration but a *suspension* of the ego; the receptive waiting that allows truth to enter. For Simone, the highest moral and cognitive act.
- **Decreation** — The voluntary undoing of the self to make room for God/reality; not destruction but *un-selfing*.
- **Affliction** (*Malheur*) — Extreme suffering that strips away illusion and false consolation; a access point to the real.
- **Necessity** — The blind, mechanical order of the world (akin to gravity); what simply *is*, without goodness or malice.
- **The Good** — That which lies beyond necessity; what we orient toward through attention and love.
- **Metaxu** — The Platonic "in-between": the finite as ladder to the infinite; material reality as both veil and bridge.
- **Roots** / **Rootedness** (*Enracinement*) — The spiritual need for belonging to a place, tradition, and community.

### Social & Epistemic Terms
- **Obedience** — Following the real as it presents itself, whether in factory work, intellectual inquiry, or prayer.
- **The Impersonal** — The transcendence of ego in favor of what is objectively required.
- **Void** — The experience of absence at the center of desire; not nihilism but the clearing where grace might enter.
