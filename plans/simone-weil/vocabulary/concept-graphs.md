## V. Master Insight

All three graphs converge on a single move: **stop calculating, start seeing form.** Noether's *Denken statt Rechnen*, André's *Analogy*, and Simone's *Attention* are three names for the same operation — the suspension of the ego's need to manipulate, replaced by a receptive recognition of structure.

What differs is what each thinker applies this operation to:

| Thinker | Applied to | Finds |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Noether | Algebraic expressions | Structural relations |
| André Weil | Mathematical domains | Hidden correspondences |
| Simone Weil | The self | Necessity and the Good |
