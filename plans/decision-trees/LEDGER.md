# Claims ledger: decision-trees

This series hasn't had a full fact-check; these are settled leads only (from plans/FACT-CHECK.md, 2026-09-27).

| page | claim | verdict | source or derivation | fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 05, index | TreeSHAP credited to "Lundberg (2017)" | wrong, fixed | The 2017 paper (Lundberg and Lee, NeurIPS, "A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions") introduced SHAP and KernelSHAP. Exact polynomial-time TreeSHAP is Lundberg, Erion and Lee, "Consistent Individualized Feature Attribution for Tree Ensembles", arXiv:1802.03888 (2018); journal version Lundberg et al., Nature Machine Intelligence 2, 56-67 (2020) | 05 now credits Lundberg, Erion and Lee (2018); index references list SHAP (2017) and TreeSHAP (2018) separately |
| 02 | "Oblique trees (1994)" | wrong, fixed | Breiman, Friedman, Olshen and Stone, CART (1984), sec. 5.2, already offered linear-combination splits; 1994 is OC1 (Murthy, Kasif and Salzberg, JAIR 2, 1-32), which searches for them by randomized hill climbing | Sentence now credits CART (1984) and describes OC1 (1994) as the randomized search |
| 05 | Gain ratio "drops the ID column's score to near zero"; caption calls age weak, job informative, city weakly informative | wrong, fixed | Ran the page's makeDataset and gain functions in node for N = 30..400: user_id gain ratio is 0.143 at N = 120 (gain about 1 over split entropy log2 N), 0.20 at N = 30, 0.11 at N = 400, above age at roughly half the N values and always above job and city. Generator: age < 40 moves P(label) 0.3 to 0.7 (strong), job shifts it by 0.1 (weak), city is unused | Prose now says gain ratio divides by log2 N, about 0.14 at N = 120, a cut not a knockout; caption fixes the feature strengths and says user_id lands level with age and ahead of job and city; PROMPT updated |
| 01 | "Pure-leaf case" preset ties the "Balanced case" under misclassification | wrong, fixed | The presets drew labels at random (seeds 13, 23), so counts drifted off 15/5 + 5/15 and 10/20 + 10/0; the first right-side point also sat at x = 0.5 and fell left of the x <= 0.5 split | Presets now build exact counts (shuffled within each side) and start the right side past the threshold; checked in node: both give misclassification child impurity 0.25, Gini 0.375 vs 0.333, entropy 0.811 vs 0.689 |
| 03 | Fig 2 caption: "Extra-Trees is m = 1 with 100%" | wrong, fixed | Geurts, Ernst and Wehenkel, "Extremely randomized trees," Machine Learning 63 (2006): default K = sqrt(p) for classification, K = p for regression, no bootstrap; K = 1 is "totally randomized trees" | Caption: Extra-Trees (regression default, every feature) is m = 4 with 100%, notes Extra-Trees skip the bootstrap, and names m = 1 as totally randomized trees |
| 06 | A missed leaf's true uplift "is the background +8pp" | wrong, fixed | Ran HonestCore.run for seeds 0..399 at the defaults (160 per half, 36pp): of 197 runs whose chosen leaf missed the persuadable corner, the true uplift is median 4.8pp, 180 fall between 4 and 8pp, 16 are exactly 8pp; leaves that take in the 85%-buy corner (uplift 0) are diluted | "usually just 4 to 8pp: the +8pp background, diluted wherever the leaf takes in part of the buy-anyway corner" |
