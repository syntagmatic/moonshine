The Grateful Dead: 2,358 Shows

Seven interactive explorations of 30 years of Grateful Dead concerts, built from the gdshowsdb database of every show from 1965 to 1995.

The Grateful Dead played 2,358 shows over 30 years, performing 436 unique songs a total of 39,774 times. No two shows were the same. These explorations use setlist data to surface the patterns behind the improvisation: how the repertoire evolved, which venues the band returned to, what grammar governed their setlists, how songs flowed through segues, which songs traveled as companions, and how six distinct eras shaped the Dead's identity.

Each piece stands alone, start anywhere. Data from Jef Smith's gdshowsdb, a database of every known Grateful Dead performance.
