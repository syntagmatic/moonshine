Construction A Builder

Pick eight integers whose parities match a Hamming codeword. Hit the target squared length. Eight challenges per round.

Prerequisite. Drills E8 series explainer #14, E8 as an Error-Correcting Code. Go to explainer.
How it works.
  1. Each challenge gives you a Hamming codeword (eight bits) and a target squared length.
  2. Type an integer into each of the eight coordinate cells. Each integer's parity (even or odd) must match the codeword bit above it — 0 wants even, 1 wants odd.
  3. The squared length of your vector is . Make it equal the target.
  4. Press Submit (or hit Enter) to lock in. Eight challenges per round.

Construction A Builder. The lattice Λ(C) consists of all integer vectors whose coordinate parities form a codeword of the extended Hamming [8, 4, 4] code. Each round drills the move from "codeword + target length" to "explicit lattice vector," the fundamental step of Construction A. Tutorial fixes the codeword to the all-zero word so only the squared-length constraint is active. Fluency walks the full 16 codewords. Challenge adds a 30-second timer.

Why this game exists

Reading the explainer once is enough to understand Construction A — codeword in, lattice vector out. But the move from a parity pattern to a specific integer 8-tuple of a chosen squared length lives somewhere else in the brain. This game forces the move enough times that the pattern becomes a reflex: weight-4 codewords accept ±1 in the four "1" positions for ‖v‖² = 4; the zero codeword accepts a single ±2 for ‖v‖² = 4 or two ±1's perpendicular to it as sums; the all-ones word lives at ‖v‖² = 8 and above. After a few rounds the reader stops translating "codeword to vector" and starts seeing them as the same object.
