B3 — Octavian Unit Hunt

An 8-vector appears. Decide whether it is one of the 240 octavian integer units — the E8 roots realized as unit octonions — before the timer runs out.

Prerequisite. This game bridges the Octonions and E8 series. The 240 octavian units are the 240 roots of E8 realized as integer octonions. Helpful reading: the E8 series explainer #03 Meet E8, the 240 Roots and (when it lands) the Octonions explainer on octavian integers.

Each candidate is an 8-vector. Decide whether it's an octavian unit by checking two things: the squared length is exactly 2, and every coordinate belongs to one of the two allowed families — either all integer with exactly two nonzero ±1 entries, or all half-integer with an even number of minus signs. Anything else is a trap.
