Parallel Coordinates

Twenty-two interactive explorations of Inselberg's geometric framework, from the duality that makes it all work to exceptional root systems and the connections that emerged along the way.

A point in five-dimensional space is impossible to picture. But draw five parallel axes and connect the values with a polyline, and the point becomes visible. More than visible: the geometric relationships between dimensions become readable. Correlations appear as crossing patterns. Clusters emerge as bundles of lines. Hyperplanes leave structured signatures.

Alfred Inselberg spent decades developing the mathematical foundations that explain why this works. These explorations make that geometry interactive. Drag points, brush axes, rotate polytopes, and build intuition for structures that exist beyond three dimensions.

The Vocabulary

Six colors recur across the series, each tied to a geometric role. The card thumbnails above use the same palette, so a blue line is always a polyline, a red dot is always a convergence or crossing, and so on.

polyline
The image of a point — a broken line that zigzags across every axis.
data lines · individual observations · cluster bundles · outlier signatures
point
A Cartesian dot — vertices, reference positions, indexed-point pivots.
polytope vertices · indexed points · group elements in Cayley graphs · lattice sites
crossing
Where polylines intersect — the place correlations and convergences become visible.
dual-image points · convergence patterns · highlighted anomalies · Pareto fronts
brush
The viewer's selection — what gets highlighted when you drag on an axis.
axis brushes · strum brushes · selected subsets · interactive filters
surface
An envelope bounding a family of polylines — hypersurfaces, trade-off curves, workspaces.
tangent envelopes · Pareto fronts · collision envelopes · reachable workspaces · hstar regions
axis
The structural backbone — the parallel rails against which everything else is plotted.
parallel axes · coordinate axes · reordering handles · permutation controls
