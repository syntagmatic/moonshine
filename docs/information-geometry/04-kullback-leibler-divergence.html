The direction of KL matters significantly in practice. Minimizing forward KL encourages "mode-covering" behavior (often leading to blurry generation, common in VAEs), while minimizing reverse KL encourages "mode-seeking" or mode-collapse behavior (common in GANs).

Concept Summary

  • KL Divergence: Measures the excess description length required when using a suboptimal code, or the statistical surprise.
  • Asymmetry is a Feature: The lack of symmetry encodes the real-world difference between approximating a sharp true distribution with a blurry model vs. a blurry true distribution with a sharp model.
  • Divergence vs. Metric: Because it lacks symmetry and the triangle inequality, KL is a divergence, not a metric. However, its local (infinitesimal) behavior recovers the Fisher metric.
