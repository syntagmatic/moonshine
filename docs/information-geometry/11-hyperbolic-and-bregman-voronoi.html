Hyperbolic and Bregman Voronoi Diagrams

Part 11 of 15. A Voronoi diagram cuts a space into cells by nearest-neighbour membership. On a statistical manifold, the cells warp into shapes that a Euclidean eye would call impossible, and the shapes are exactly right for clustering distributions.

The Core Concept

A Voronoi diagram partitions a space by assigning every point to its "nearest" site. But on a statistical manifold, what does "nearest" mean? If you use Euclidean distance, you get straight lines and convex polygons, but this fundamentally misunderstands the geometry of probability. If we instead define distance using a Bregman divergence (like KL) or a Riemannian geodesic distance (like Fisher-Rao), the Voronoi cells warp to reflect the true geometry of the space:

  • KL Voronoi Diagram: Bisectors curve due to the asymmetry of Kullback-Leibler divergence. It is the statistically correct choice for most exponential families.
  • Fisher-Rao Voronoi Diagram: Cells warp strongly toward the boundary, mirroring the classic hyperbolic tiling of the Poincaré half-plane. Small- cells extend short distances in but long distances in .

Because KL is a Bregman divergence, the KL Voronoi diagram on a curved manifold is mathematically equivalent to a flat power diagram in expectation coordinates. This means we can compute these seemingly complex curved partitions just as fast as standard Euclidean ones.

Concept Summary

  • Geometry Dictates Shape: A Voronoi diagram is a discretised picture of a metric. Euclidean, KL, and Fisher-Rao metrics produce entirely different decision boundaries for the exact same points.
  • Bregman Clustering: This isn't just theory; it's the foundation of k-means clustering. Classical k-means uses the Euclidean Voronoi diagram. For data that represents probability distributions (e.g., histograms, document word counts), you should use Bregman k-means.
  • Computational Magic: Despite living on a curved manifold, Bregman Voronoi diagrams can be computed efficiently (in time) by leveraging the Legendre transform to map them back to flat space.
