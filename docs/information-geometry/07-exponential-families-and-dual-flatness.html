Exponential Families and Dual Flatness

The categorical row is the exponential family hiding behind the softmax function of deep learning. If you have ever wondered why the softmax plays so well with cross-entropy loss, the geometric answer is that softmax converts natural parameters into expectation parameters (via ) and cross-entropy is the KL divergence that respects the dually flat geometry of the categorical family. Natural gradient descent on a softmax, which we meet in Part 10, is simply re-expressing gradient descent in the expectation coordinates where the geometry is flat.

What "dually flat" buys you. Because the natural parameters are e-flat and the expectation parameters are m-flat, you get two coordinate systems in which one of the two connections has vanishing Christoffel symbols. Geodesics in θ are straight lines; geodesics in η are straight lines. The curvature you worked so hard to understand in Act I — the hyperbolic bending of the Poincaré half-plane — is a property of the Levi-Civita connection, not the e- or m-connections. As soon as you flip to exponential-family coordinates, the manifold becomes flat. The Levi-Civita curvature survives, but it no longer gets in the way of computation.
