Concept Summary

  • The Fisher Metric: Translates coordinate changes into statistical distinguishability.
  • Geodesics are curved: The shortest path between distributions (the Fisher geodesic) is rarely a straight line in parameter space. Linear interpolation is statistically misleading.
  • Parameterisation Invariance: Under any smooth reparameterisation , the metric transforms correctly as a tensor, ensuring that distances remain invariant.
