Bioinformatics Visualization

The standard plots of modern bioinformatics, from genome browsers to survival curves, read through one example: how losing the tumor suppressor TP53 can leave its target p21 switched off.

Biology now measures thousands of cells, millions of DNA bases and whole patient cohorts at once, and bioinformatics has built a set of standard plots for reading data of that size.

The series follows the tumor suppressor TP53 and its target CDKN1A (p21). The first essay looks at them through five kinds of data at once. The rest take one kind each: coordinates and reads on the genome, genome-wide rearrangements, where p53 binds DNA, which genes change expression, how genes and patients cluster, how cells are mapped in two dimensions, and what the evidence says about patients. All data is simulated in the page, and each essay says what it computes.

The vocabulary

Five semantic colors recur across the techniques.

gene
A genomic feature being measured, a locus, transcript, or variant.
gene bodies · exons · SNPs · protein domains
sample
An individual patient, cell, or replicate, one observation in the cohort.
heatmap columns · oncoprint tracks · single cells
expression
Measured signal intensity, how much of something there is.
read counts · log-fold-change · heatmap cell values · peak height
significance
Statistical thresholds and called hits, what rises above the noise floor.
p-values · FDR cutoffs · volcano hits · ChIP-seq peaks
pathway
Structural or functional connections, the wiring between features.
pathway edges · ChIP-seq binding · chord links
