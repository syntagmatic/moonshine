Waterfall Plot

Ranking patients by CDKN1A expression to visualize the impact of TP53 mutations across a cohort.

Previous explainers in this series looked at TP53 as a master regulator and CDKN1A as one of its primary downstream targets. To see how this relationship plays out across a population, we use a waterfall plot, which ranks every sample in a cohort along the x-axis to create a cascading visualization of quantitative data.

The power of the waterfall plot lies in its ability to reveal associations between a continuous variable and a categorical one. By sorting by expression and coloring by mutation status, we can instantly see if a specific genotype clusters at one end of the phenotypic spectrum.

Figure 1. CDKN1A expression waterfall for 60 patients. The bars are sorted descending. TP53 mutant samples (red) cluster toward the low-expression end, as TP53 is a transcriptional activator of CDKN1A.

CDKN1A (p21) is a cell cycle inhibitor. When TP53 is mutated, it loses the ability to activate CDKN1A, leading to decreased expression in mutant tumors and potentially faster cell division.

Sorting by Dimension

A cohort can be reordered by different measures to reveal different stories. Tumor Mutation Burden (TMB) is often elevated in mutant tumors due to impaired DNA repair, a second ordering axis that tells a different story on the same patients.

Figure 2. Reshuffling the cohort. TP53 mutant samples cluster at the low end of CDKN1A but often occupy the high end of the TMB spectrum, illustrating the multi-faceted impact of a single driver mutation.
