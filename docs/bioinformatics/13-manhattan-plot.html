Manhattan Plot

The skyline of the genome: scanning millions of variants to find disease risk loci.

A GWAS tests genetic variants for links to disease. The Manhattan plot arranges variants by genomic position and significance (\(-\log_{10}(p)\)). Significant loci spike upward like skyscrapers.

Figure 1. Genome-wide Manhattan plot. Strong spikes appear near TP53 (chr17) and CDKN1A (chr6).
