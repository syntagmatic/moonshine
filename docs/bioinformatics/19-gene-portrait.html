Gene Portrait

Integrating expression, mutation, pathway, and survival data into a single multidimensional profile.

Every previous explainer in this series examined one dimension of the TP53 / CDKN1A axis. We have looked at expression changes, mutation landscapes, pathway wiring, and clinical outcomes. This final synthesis assembles these views into a single Gene Portrait.

A gene is not a single measurement; it is a composite of all these dimensions. By toggling between wild-type and mutant states, we can watch how a single genomic alteration ripples through every layer of the biological system.
