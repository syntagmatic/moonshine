ChIP-seq Peak Visualization

Measuring where a protein binds the genome, and what happens when it cannot.

ChIP-seq identifies the binding sites of transcription factors. TP53 is a transcription factor that normally binds the promoter of CDKN1A to activate cell-cycle arrest.

The Binding Evidence

Below is a simulated window around the CDKN1A promoter. Wildtype TP53 (blue) shows strong binding, while the R175H mutant (red) fails to bind.

Figure 1. ChIP-seq signal at the CDKN1A locus.

Peak Calling

Computational tools distinguish real binding from noise. A peak caller assigns each candidate a significance score, typically expressed as \(-\log_{10}(p)\).
