DNA Methylation

Is CDKN1A silenced by promoter methylation in TP53-mutant tumors?

When TP53 is functional, it drives CDKN1A expression. But there is a second way a cell can silence a gene: DNA methylation. When CpG sites in a promoter are heavily methylated, transcription factors cannot bind, and the gene goes silent.

The beta value (\(\beta\)) measures the ratio of methylated signal to total signal, ranging from 0 (unmethylated) to 1 (fully methylated).
