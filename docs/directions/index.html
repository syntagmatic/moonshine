Future Directions

24 interactive explorations of where two sibling projects could go next: the moonshine explainer series, and the d3-power-tools library it's built on.

These directions are organized by whose roadmap they sit on. Future of moonshine extends the explanation-authoring loop — narrative grammars, templates, effectiveness, cognitive accessibility. Future of d3-power-tools belongs to the charting stack underneath — data backends, portable components, print export. Shared ground holds directions that load-bear for both: a single article that serves both roadmaps.

Each is a self-contained interactive explanation that demonstrates the concept it proposes. They range from near-term additions (Observable Plot bridge, print export) to research frontiers (explanation effectiveness, skill ecology) — practical extensions mixed with open questions presented as interactive arguments for the reader.

The Vocabulary

Six colors recur across the explorations above, each tied to a layer of the explanation-making stack. Per-card accents and glyph colors match — so a green card and a green glyph both belong to the authoring layer, and a red card and a red sparkle both belong to the AI layer. The three section dots (green, blue, purple) mark project roadmaps and are independent of the per-card layer colors.
