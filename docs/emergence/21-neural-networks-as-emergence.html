Neural Networks as Emergence

Billions of simple weighted sums produce the behavior we recognize as intelligence.

A single artificial neuron is simple. It takes inputs, multiplies them by weights, adds a bias, and applies a curve. This arithmetic is the building block of modern AI. Intelligence does not live in any single unit. It emerges from the collective pattern of connections.

1. The Decision Line

A neuron computes sigmoid(w1*x1 + w2*x2 + b). This formula draws a straight line in the input space. Everything on one side is "on," and everything on the other is "off." Weights rotate the line; bias shifts it.
