Collective Construction

Complexity does not require a designer. It emerges from agents responding to local cues in their environment.

Paper wasps build intricate nests without a plan. Each wasp follows local rules: if a surface looks like this, add a pellet of wood pulp. This coordination through the environment is called stigmergy.

1. Local Rules, Global Nest

In the simulation below, builders walk randomly. When they encounter a pattern that matches their rules, they deposit a block. Watch as arches and chambers emerge from a simple foundation.

Figure 1. Stigmergic construction in action. Builders read local neighborhood patterns to decide where to place blocks.

2. The Architecture of Rules

Different rules produce different structures. Toggle rules to see how the "design" changes.

Figure 2. The construction rule set. Brown is solid, white is empty, gray is "any."

Collective construction is self-healing. If a section of the nest is destroyed, the broken edges create the same local patterns that triggered the original build, prompting the wasps to repair it.
