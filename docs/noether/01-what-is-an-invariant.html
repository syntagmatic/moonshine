What Is an Invariant?

Part 1 of 13. Emmy Noether's work in physics and algebra keeps asking what stays fixed when a group acts. This part sets up the vocabulary: group actions, orbits, stabilizers and invariant functions.

This series follows one instinct of Emmy Noether's: to understand a system, find what stays the same when you act on it, and let that do the work.

Only stays flat, and with it anything computed from it, such as the distance to the origin. The rotations form a group: two rotations compose to a rotation, each can be undone, and rotating by zero does nothing. An invariant of the group is a quantity that every member leaves alone.
