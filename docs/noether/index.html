Invariance

The Mathematics of Emmy Noether — a fifteen-part interactive series on the principle that ties physics and abstract algebra into one career.

Emmy Noether (1882–1935) is the mathematician who taught the twentieth century how to see invariance. In physics, her first theorem turned every continuous symmetry of an action into a conservation law: time translation gives energy, rotation gives angular momentum, phase rotation gives electric charge. In algebra, her chain-condition framework made commutative algebra and algebraic geometry possible in their modern form. This series follows both legacies through worked examples — the same instinct, expressed in two different mathematical languages.

Three acts, fifteen explainers. Each acts on its own; the three together build a single picture of what "invariance" can do.
