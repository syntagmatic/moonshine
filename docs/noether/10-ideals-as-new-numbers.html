From Kummer to Noether

Dedekind proved unique factorization into prime ideals for the ring of integers of every number field. Noether's Idealtheorie in Ringbereichen (1921) worked in any commutative ring with the ascending chain condition, where every ideal is an intersection of finitely many primary ideals. In 1927 she showed Dedekind's theorem holds exactly in the Noetherian, integrally closed rings whose nonzero primes are maximal: the Dedekind domains. The next part states the chain condition.
