The three classical triples

Together with explainer 6, we have the three oldest conservation laws in physics, each delivered by Noether's first theorem as the current of a specific continuous symmetry of the Lagrangian.

SymmetryTransformationGeneratorConserved currentName
Time homogeneity — Energy
Space homogeneity Linear momentum
Space isotropy Angular momentum

Three of the most celebrated results in physics — Newton's first law, conservation of linear momentum, conservation of angular momentum — are corollaries of a single theorem and a single observation: that space and time are featureless. When spacetime stops being featureless (a gravitational field, a non-uniform background) the corresponding currents stop being conserved, in exactly the direction the broken symmetry points.

Takeaways

  • Translation invariance gives as a Noether current.
  • Rotation invariance around an axis gives the corresponding angular momentum component.
  • Partial symmetries give partial conservation: horizontal translations survive a vertical gravitational field; horizontal momentum is still conserved.
  • No continuous symmetry, no Noether current — the quadrupole figure is the converse in action.
