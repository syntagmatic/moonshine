Least Action and Lagrangians

Part 3 of 13. Noether's theorem is about systems described by a Lagrangian, a function whose integral along the path actually taken is stationary. This part sets up that framework and measures it.

Noether's theorem is about a group acting on a physical system, and the system is specified by a Lagrangian . Its integral along a path is the action, and the path actually followed makes the action stationary.

Which curve is fastest?

In June 1696 Johann Bernoulli asked the readers of the Acta Eruditorum which curve between two points at different heights carries a bead, released from rest, from one to the other in the least time. Leibniz, Jakob Bernoulli, Tschirnhaus, l'Hôpital and (anonymously) Newton all found the answer, a cycloid. What is minimized is a number attached to a whole curve,

with the bead's speed fixed by energy conservation.
