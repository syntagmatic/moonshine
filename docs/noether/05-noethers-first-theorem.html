Noether's First Theorem

Part 5 of 13. Every continuous symmetry of a Lagrangian gives a quantity that stays constant along the motion. The proof is five lines; the figures measure it.

Part 4 sorted transformations into strict symmetries, which leave unchanged, and quasi-symmetries, which change it by a total derivative . Either kind gives a quantity

that is constant along every solution of the equations of motion. This is the first theorem of Noether's 1918 paper Invariante Variationsprobleme (Nachr. Ges. Wiss. Göttingen, 235–257), stated there for fields as well as particles.
