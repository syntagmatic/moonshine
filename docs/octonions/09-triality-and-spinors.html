Triality and Spinors

Part 9 of 15. In 8 dimensions, the distinction between "directions" and "states" dissolves. Vectors and spinors become interchangeable.

In 3D physics, vectors and spinors are fundamentally different. If you rotate a vector 360°, it returns to normal. But a spinor (like an electron) must be rotated 720° to return to its original state. They inhabit different worlds.

But 8 dimensions is a geometric miracle. Here, there is a perfect three-way symmetry between vectors ($V$), left-handed spinors ($S_L$), and right-handed spinors ($S_R$). This is **Triality**, and it is the reason Octonions are the bridge to Superstring theory.

Mission: The Triality Balancer

The triality rule states that if we treat all three spaces as Octonions, their product must be conserved: $v \times s_L = s_R$. If you rotate the vector space, you must "balance" the other two to keep the universe intact. Your goal is to find the balancing rotations for $S_L$ and $S_R$ after a vector shift.
