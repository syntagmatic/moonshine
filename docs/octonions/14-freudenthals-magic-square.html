Freudenthal’s Magic Square

Part 14 of 15. The Periodic Table of symmetry. By combining Octonions with other algebras, we generate the most complex structures in the universe.

There are five "exceptional" Lie groups ($G_2, F_4, E_6, E_7, E_8$). They are called exceptional because they don't fit into the standard families of rotations. They are unique, monolithic peaks of symmetry. For a long time, they seemed like random accidents of math.

But Hans Freudenthal and Jacques Tits discovered a "Magic Square" that builds them all from the four normed division algebras ($R, C, H, O$).

Mission: The Symmetry Constructor

The Magic Square works by "pairing" two algebras. One algebra determines the type of space, and the other determines the type of number. Your goal is to combine **Octonions** with other systems to see how the exceptional groups grow in size and complexity.
