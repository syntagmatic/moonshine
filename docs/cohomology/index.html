Holes Have Names

Six interactive explainers on cohomology — the algebraic shadow of every hole. From a 1-cocycle on a triangulated torus to a persistent cohomology barcode, with the cup product as the move that turns groups into a ring you can compute on.

Homology counts holes. Cohomology gives those holes names you can add, multiply, and pair against chains. This series builds the algebra topology-first: a small triangulation, a coboundary you can apply by hand, a cup product table you can fill in cell by cell — then de Rham, Mayer–Vietoris, and persistent cohomology as three concrete ways to compute the same object.
