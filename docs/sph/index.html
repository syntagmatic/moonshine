Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics

Building a particle fluid, then moving it onto the GPU.

Smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH) simulates a fluid as a collection of particles. Each particle estimates the local density from its neighbors, turns density into pressure, and feels pressure and viscosity forces from the particles around it.

The first part builds that loop one term at a time, from the smoothing kernel to a small fluid pouring into a container around obstacles. The second part makes it fast: a spatial hash so each particle only checks nearby cells, and WebGPU compute passes that update every particle at once.
